This post summarizes my **Thermal Dynamics** project—a comprehensive research paper analyzing heat dissipation mechanisms in modern heatsink designs. Computational fluid dynamics simulations were coupled with experimental validation to find optimal thermal performance.

The work used SimScale and related CFD tooling to compare fin geometries, airflow patterns, and material choices under realistic load conditions.
