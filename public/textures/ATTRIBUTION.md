# Planet texture attribution

Planet surface textures and inspiration for the WebGL scene draw from publicly licensed sources:

- [Solar System Scope textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) — CC BY 4.0
- NASA Blue Marble imagery — public domain

3D planet layering patterns adapted from MIT-licensed references:

- [yuvrajraina/tinyearth](https://github.com/yuvrajraina/tinyearth) (Earth atmosphere layers)
- [jjteoh-thewebdev/r3f-solar-system](https://github.com/jjteoh-thewebdev/r3f-solar-system) (sphere + ring geometry)

Colors are tinted to match the portfolio palette (`#208bd2`, `#64ffda`, `#fdd835`, `#1e3a8a`, `#d97706`).
